# Code of Conduct

DialGlide contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, professional, and focused on the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data.
- Accept reasonable moderation and technical review.
- Give credit for work and sources.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, tracking, credential collection, or misleading behavior.
- Spam, unrelated promotion, or repeated disruption.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@receive-smss.live`. Include only the minimum information needed to review the concern. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official DialGlide community spaces.
